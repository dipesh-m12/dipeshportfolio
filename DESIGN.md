# Design decisions

The first screen establishes Dipesh as a builder who connects product ideas to working systems. Oversized type leads; an original orange-and-chrome sculpture gives that idea a physical expression. Selected work follows immediately, then personal context, expertise, and a direct contact invitation.

The primary action is Explore my work. Each project opens a focused detail panel with capabilities, technology, and a real destination. Filters serve visitors interested specifically in platforms, AI, or creative technology.

## System

- Paper `#f4f3ed`, ink `#20211d`, secondary surface `#e9e9df`.
- Orange `#ef4b23` for large emphasis and accent surfaces. Dark orange `#bc3515` for smaller accent text on light surfaces.
- Muted copy `#66675f`; lines `#d5d6cc` are decorative separators.
- Manrope for titles and body, Georgia italic for expressive emphasis, Courier New for small metadata.
- Four/eight-pixel spacing rhythm, with 24–32px component spacing and 56–94px section spacing.
- Desktop split layouts collapse into one column on narrow screens. The mobile 3D scene follows the text and primary action.
- Motion responds to interaction and pauses when not visible. Reduced-motion settings suppress transitions and automatic 3D movement.

The forest image is original AI-generated concept art. The Rizzerv visual is an interface study with a small native CSS salon illustration, preserving clarity about what is illustrative. No generated portrait or unrelated stock person is presented as Dipesh.
